Common questions

Where is Settings?

On the My Family screen (the list of your family members), tap the gear button in the top-left corner.

How do I back up my records?
  1. Open Settings › Back up now.
  2. Choose a password. Write it down somewhere safe; nobody, including us, can recover it.
  3. Save the backup file to the Files app, a computer, or another place you trust.

Make a new backup now and then, especially after adding a lot of records.

How do I move my records to a new iPhone?
  1. On your old iPhone, make a backup (see above) and save it somewhere the new iPhone can reach, or AirDrop it.
  2. On the new iPhone, install Family Health Binder and open Settings › Restore from a backup.
  3. Pick the backup file and enter its password.

Restoring replaces whatever is already in the app on that iPhone. If you set up your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one, your records usually come along automatically.

Can my partner or a caregiver see our records on their iPhone?

Yes. Open Settings › Share family members…, pick who to share, set a password and send the file by AirDrop, Messages or email. On their iPhone, they save the file to Files, then open Settings › Import shared family members. Tell them the password in person or by phone, not in the same message as the file.

This is a copy, not live syncing. Changes made on one iPhone don't appear on the other until you share again.

Why doesn't it sync with iCloud?

Apple's App Store rules don't allow apps to store personal health information in iCloud, and keeping records only on your iPhone is also the most private option. Encrypted backup and share files give you a way to move records when you need to.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Nothing is deleted. You can still view, copy, share and export every record. To add or edit records again, buy the one-time unlock for $6.99. There's no subscription.

I already paid. How do I unlock on another iPhone?

Install the app on the other iPhone with the same Apple Account, open the unlock screen and tap Restore purchase. With Apple Family Sharing turned on, people in your Family Sharing group can do the same with their own Apple Account.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all App Store payments. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

A scan filled in something wrong.

Scans are read on your iPhone, and the app always shows you what it found before saving. Fix anything that looks off, or tap the record afterward to edit it. Clear, flat, well-lit pictures read best. If a type of form never reads well, email us a description (not a picture) of what went wrong.

Face ID isn't working.

You can always use your iPhone passcode instead. If Face ID stopped working for the app, check iPhone Settings › Face ID & Passcode › Other Apps and make sure Family Health Binder is turned on.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app from your iPhone. That removes every record stored in it. Any backup files you saved elsewhere are separate, so delete those too if you want.

Medical disclaimer

Family Health Binder is a place to keep your own records. It does not give medical advice. Always check with your doctor or pharmacist before making medical decisions. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
