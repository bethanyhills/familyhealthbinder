How it works

Set it up once on a quiet evening. It pays off at every appointment after that.

  1. Add your family

    Create a profile for each person with the basics: birthday, height, weight and insurance.

  2. Fill in the records

    Type them in, pick from lists, or scan the paperwork you already have.

  3. Open it at the front desk

    Pull up the intake summary and copy what the form asks for, or hand over a PDF.

Private by design

There is no account to create and no server holding your family's health information. We can't see your records, and neither can anyone else.
