Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

The short version: Family Health Binder collects nothing. Your family's records are stored only on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never see your records.

Family Health Binder is an iPhone app for keeping your own family's medical records. This policy explains what happens to the information you put in it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no server and no sign-up. It does not send your records, your usage or any identifiers to us or to anyone else. On the App Store, the app's privacy label is "Data Not Collected."

Where your records are stored

Everything you enter, including scans and photos of documents and insurance cards, is stored only on your iPhone. iOS encrypts the app's files whenever your iPhone is locked, and the app asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode each time it opens.

Family Health Binder does not sync your records to iCloud. Like other app data, your records are included in your iPhone's own device backup (iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer) if you have that turned on. Those backups are run by Apple and your device settings, not by us.

Scanning documents

When you scan a prescription label, visit summary, vaccine record, school plan, business card or insurance card, the text is read on your iPhone using Apple's built-in text recognition. The picture and the text are not uploaded anywhere.

Camera and photos

The app asks to use the camera or your photo library only so you can scan or attach pictures. Pictures stay on your iPhone.

Pharmacy search

When you search for a pharmacy, the words you type (for example, "CVS Main Street") are sent to Apple Maps to find matching stores. That search is not linked to you or to any health information, and we never receive it.

Backups and sharing

Your records leave your iPhone only when you choose to send them:

  • Backup files are encrypted with a password you choose. You decide where to save them, such as the Files app or a computer. We can't open them, and we can't recover a forgotten password.
  • Shared family member files are encrypted with a password you choose, and contain only the people you pick and the providers, insurance, pharmacies, contacts and schools they use.
  • PDF summaries you export are not encrypted. Share them only with people you trust, such as a doctor's office.

When you send any of these by AirDrop, Messages, Mail or another app, that app's own privacy policy applies.

Feedback emails

If you send feedback from the app, it opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. The message includes the app version, your iOS version and your device type so we can help, and nothing from your records. We receive only what you choose to send, and we use it only to answer you and improve the app. Please don't include medical details in emails. Ask us any time to delete your emails.

Purchases

The free trial is tracked on your iPhone. If you buy the one-time unlock, Apple handles the payment. We do not receive your name or payment details.

Children

Parents can keep records for their children in the app. Because nothing is collected, we do not collect information from or about children.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect its service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Deleting your data

Because your records live only on your iPhone, you are in control. Delete a family member in the app, or delete the app to remove everything. Backup files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete.

Medical disclaimer

Family Health Binder does not give medical advice. Always check with your doctor or pharmacist before making medical decisions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
